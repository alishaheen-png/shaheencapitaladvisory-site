Shaheen Capital Advisory

Capital advisory for UAE businesses

Shaheen Capital Advisory is a capital advisory business working with companies in the UAE. We advise businesses on the capital options available to them and make introductions where they are useful.

What we do

We work alongside a business to understand its position, then help it identify the capital paths that are actually relevant to it. We are advisers and introducers. We do not lend, and we do not act as agent for any lender.

How it works

Every business sits in a different place, so we start with the business in front of us — what it does, what it needs, and where it is in its journey. From there we help map the relevant options and support the business as it works through them.
